Why go solar with SolarWave

Daytime power you own, and backup for when the grid lets you down.

Common questions

Before you fill in the form.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. The site visit and the written proposal are free, with no obligation to proceed.

Will my solar system work during a brownout?

A grid-tie system shuts off during an outage for safety. A hybrid system with batteries keeps your essential loads running. Tell us what matters most in the form and we'll recommend the right setup.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on your bill, your roof and whether you want battery backup. The free assessment gives you an exact price for your property instead of a guess.

What is net metering?

Net metering lets you send excess daytime power back to the grid in exchange for credit on your bill. We'll walk you through the application with your utility.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installs take one to three days once the design is approved and materials are on site.

Which areas do you serve?

Enter your city and province in the form and we'll confirm coverage when we call you.
